I study how to evaluate multilingual language technologies, especially for low-resource languages, and what we learn by involving the people who build and use them.

Research

My current work asks when automatic metrics reflect how useful a language technology is to people. In an ongoing cross-lingual question answering project, I evaluate machine translations with automatic metrics and model-based question answering. I also built a web-based study to test whether people can answer comprehension questions using those translations.

My earlier work includes interviews with practitioners developing tools for low-resource Sino-Tibetan languages, a survey of NLP research on those languages, and experiments on how well named entity recognition models generalize over time.

About
